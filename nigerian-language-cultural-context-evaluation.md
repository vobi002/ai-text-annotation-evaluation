Nigerian Language & Cultural Context AI Evaluation

Project Overview

This project demonstrates practical skills in evaluating AI-generated interpretations of Nigerian English and Nigerian Pidgin expressions.

The goal is to assess whether an AI system correctly understands not only the literal meaning of Nigerian expressions, but also their contextual, pragmatic, and cultural meaning.

Evaluation Focus

The dataset evaluates AI interpretations across:

* Nigerian Pidgin
* Nigerian English
* Context-dependent expressions
* Idiomatic language
* Pragmatic meaning
* Unsupported assumptions
* Literal versus contextual interpretation

Evaluation Labels

Correct

The AI interpretation accurately captures the meaning and context without introducing unsupported assumptions.

Partially Correct

The AI captures the general or literal meaning but misses an important contextual or pragmatic element, or introduces an unsupported assumption.

Incorrect

The AI substantially misunderstands the expression or its intended meaning.

Annotation Approach

For each example, the evaluator:

1. Reads the Nigerian expression.
2. Identifies the likely meaning based on context.
3. Reviews the AI interpretation.
4. Checks whether the interpretation preserves the intended meaning.
5. Identifies missed contextual or pragmatic information.
6. Checks for unsupported assumptions.
7. Assigns an evaluation label.
8. Provides a concise evaluator rationale.

Example

Expression:
“You dey come?”

AI interpretation:
“The user is asking whether you are currently coming to their location.”

Evaluation: Partially Correct

Rationale:
The AI correctly understands that the user is asking whether someone is coming, but it adds the unsupported assumption that the person is coming to the user’s location.

Key Linguistic Considerations

The evaluation recognizes that Nigerian expressions may not always be accurately interpreted through direct word-for-word translation.

For example, words such as “dey” and “na” can perform different grammatical or pragmatic functions depending on context.

Therefore, evaluation considers:

* Literal meaning
* Context
* Sentence structure
* Pragmatic meaning
* Tone where relevant
* Cultural and linguistic usage

Quality Control

Annotations were reviewed for:

* Consistent application of labels
* Unsupported assumptions
* Contextual accuracy
* Correct interpretation of Nigerian expressions
* Clear and objective rationales
* Distinction between literal and contextual meaning

Dataset

The accompanying spreadsheet contains 12 annotated examples involving Nigerian English and Nigerian Pidgin expressions.

Each example includes the expression, language variety, expected meaning, example AI judgment, evaluator rationale, and relevant linguistic or contextual notes.

Conclusion

This project demonstrates the ability to evaluate AI language understanding within a Nigerian linguistic and cultural context.

It highlights the importance of human evaluators in identifying contextual meanings, pragmatic functions, and language-specific interpretations that may be missed by systems relying primarily on literal patterns.
